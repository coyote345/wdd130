Featured Trips

Beginner

Orilla Verde Float

Drift through the peaceful stretch of the Rio Grande Gorge in the Orilla Verde Recreation Area on this relaxing float. Perfect for families with young children, first-timers, or anyone who wants to soak in the canyon scenery without intense rapids. Bighorn sheep and river birds are often spotted along the shore.

  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Difficulty: Class I–II
  • Age Minimum: 5+
  • Price: $55 per person
Intermediate

Middle Box (La Junta)

Navigate the calmer waters of the Middle Box on this half-day trip through the Rio Grande Gorge. Ideal for beginners ready to step up from a flat-water float, this stretch offers light Class II–III rapids alongside dramatic basalt cliffs and sweeping canyon views. A great next step before tackling the Taos Box.

  • Duration: Half a day (4 hours)
  • Difficulty: Class III
  • Age Minimum: 10+
  • Price: $90 per person
Advanced

Taos Box Full Day

Take on 16 miles of the most legendary whitewater in the Southwest as you descend through the heart of the Rio Grande Gorge. With 60 rapids — 13 of them Class III, IV, and IV+ — this full-day expedition winds beneath 800-foot basalt cliffs dotted with eagle nests, delivering nonstop thrills for experienced adventurers.

  • Duration: Full day (8 hours)
  • Difficulty: Class III–IV
  • Age Minimum: 14+
  • Price: $220 per person
