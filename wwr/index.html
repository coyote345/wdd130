World-Class Rapids

Ride some of the most legendary whitewater in the Southwest, carved through towering basalt canyons and rugged wilderness. From playful Class II riffles to thundering Class IV drops, our stretches of river deliver an unforgettable mix of adrenaline and scenery. Every rapid tells its own story.

Safety You Can Trust

Every trip is led by certified, highly experienced guides trained in swift-water rescue. We provide all safety equipment, conduct thorough pre-trip briefings, and maintain a perfect safety record. Your adventure starts with confidence.

Trips for Every Level

Whether you're floating with young kids or chasing Class IV thrills, we have a trip built for you. Our range of rivers spans gentle scenic floats to intense whitewater expeditions, so every guest — beginner or expert — finds their perfect adventure.
